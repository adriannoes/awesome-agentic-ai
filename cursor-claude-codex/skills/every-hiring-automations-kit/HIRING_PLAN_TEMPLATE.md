[View the reusable Notion template](https://modern-ton-234.notion.site/Every-Hiring-Plan-Template-3f8ca4f355ac811bb4c7e7ad339d6f69). The original is view-only; duplicate it into your own workspace before filling it in.

Create one approved plan per role. This blank template defines the questions to answer; its examples are not approved criteria for any role. Replace or remove bracketed fields before use. Store completed plans in the role’s restricted workspace, not in the kit repository.

## Shape of candidate

Who can do the work described above? Define job-relevant requirements and distinguish essentials from preferences. Age and other demographics are not valid criteria.

## Application questions

- Name
- Email
- LinkedIn, portfolio or personal website, when relevant
- Resume, when required
- One required telling question tied to the role’s work and approved evidence rules

Possible telling questions, to adapt rather than require universally:

- Tell us about a time you used AI to solve a real work problem. What did you try, and what changed?
- Describe something you made with AI recently and the judgment you used while making it.
- Share a work sample and explain your own contribution, if work samples are appropriate for this role.
