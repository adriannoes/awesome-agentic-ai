# /arena

## When to use

One attempt at a design or artifact would lock in the wrong shape; you want parallel candidates compared.

## Example prompt

```text
/arena take my prompt to the arena verbatim. i want to compare their proposals with yours.
```

## What you get back

N parallel attempts, a chosen base, and the strongest parts of the losers grafted into it.
