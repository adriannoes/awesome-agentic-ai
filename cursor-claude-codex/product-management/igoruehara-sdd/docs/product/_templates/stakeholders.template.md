# Stakeholder Map

> Who has **interest** or **influence** over the product (Lean Inception). Defines who to engage
> closely vs. keep informed. Update when the landscape changes.

## Interest × influence matrix
> Position each stakeholder to decide the level of engagement.

```
Influence ▲
     high │  keep satisfied       │  manage closely
          │──────────────────────┼──────────────────────
     low  │  monitor             │  keep informed
          └──────────────────────┴──────────────────────►
                 low           Interest            high
```
